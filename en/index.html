About Yuyu-Juan

Yuyu-Juan is an Okinawa soba restaurant set in a traditional Okinawan kominka (historic wooden house) surrounded by lush greenery in Nakagusuku Village.

Our broth is made from pork bones, chicken, and bonito flakes with absolutely no MSG or artificial seasonings. All meats are simmered in our homemade sauce for hours.

We were featured on TV Asahi's "Jinsei no Rakuen" (Paradise of Life) in 2010.
