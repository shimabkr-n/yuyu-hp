Allergen information

The allergen information below covers only the 8 mandatory allergens under Japanese food labeling rules: shrimp, crab, walnut, wheat, buckwheat, egg, milk and peanut.

If you have a severe allergy, please be sure to tell our staff when you visit. Small amounts may be present because cooking utensils and oil are shared.

    Good to know

    Can I pay by card?

    No. We accept cash only. Credit cards and electronic payments are not accepted.

    Can I make a reservation?

    Reservations are by phone only. There is no online booking.

    Is Okinawa soba made from buckwheat?

    No. Okinawa soba noodles are made from wheat.

    Do you have vegetarian, vegan, halal or gluten-free options?

    Vegetarian: our broth uses pork bones, chicken and bonito flakes, so please ask our staff which dishes you can eat. Vegan and halal: not available, because we use pork and pork-bone broth. Gluten-free: not available, because the noodles are wheat and soy sauce containing wheat is widely used.

    Is there parking?

    Yes, for about 15 cars.

    Is there Wi-Fi?

    No.

    About Yuyu-Juan

    Yuyu-Juan is a family-run restaurant serving Okinawa soba and local dishes in a traditional Okinawan house surrounded by trees in Nakagusuku Village.

    Our broth is made from pork bones, chicken and bonito flakes, with no MSG or artificial seasonings. Our pork belly, pig's feet and soft-bone ribs are slowly simmered in a homemade sauce.

    The name 悠愉 means taking your time to enjoy a meal and feel at ease. 樹庵 means a place surrounded by trees.

    We were featured on TV Asahi's "Jinsei no Rakuen" in 2010, and in the Okinawa Times and Ryukyu Shimpo newspapers. We reopened after a renovation on October 1, 2025.
